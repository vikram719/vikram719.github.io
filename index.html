What is an anonymous message link?

An anonymous message link is a personal URL that anyone can open to send you a message without revealing who they are. You post the link on Instagram, Snapchat or WhatsApp. Friends tap it, type something honest and hit send. The message lands in your private inbox. You see what they said, but not who said it.

It's the same idea that made apps like Sarahah and NGL popular. The difference is that a link-based tool works in any browser, so nobody has to install anything.

How it works, step by step

  1. Create your link. Pick a name on a site such as tellmeanything.link. You get a unique link.

  3. Friends send messages. They open the link, write a message and send it. No login and no name.
  4. Read your inbox. Open your dashboard to read the messages. Screenshot the good ones and post your replies.

Staying safe with anonymous messages

Anonymous messaging is fun until someone uses it to be cruel. A few habits help:

  • Share your link only with people you actually know.
  • Don't reply to messages that are threatening or try to get personal details out of you.
  • Delete your link or stop sharing it if the messages turn nasty.
  • If a message mentions self-harm or danger, talk to someone you trust or a local helpline.

Anonymous message link vs. anonymous apps

App-based tools often lock features behind a paywall, like "see who sent this" hints, and make senders install the app first. A link in the browser removes that friction, and more friends actually send something. If you want a free anonymous message site without the upsells, a link-based option is the simpler choice.
